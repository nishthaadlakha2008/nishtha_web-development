ABC University - University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

Welcome to ABC University. This handbook provides complete information about the admission process, eligibility, required documents, fees and important admission terms.

"Our admission office is committed to helping students complete the admission process smoothly and successfully."

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the eligibility criteria specified by the university for the selected course.

Mandatory Requirement: Students must submit all required documents before admission.

Application Process

Application deadline: 30 June 2026

  1. Visit the official admission website.
  2. Register using your email and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. Upload all required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Attend counselling and complete registration.

Required Documents

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • Government Identity Proof
  • Category Certificate, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum requirements a student must satisfy for admission.
Merit List
A list of students selected based on their academic performance.
Counselling
A process where students receive guidance about courses and admission.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling as a university student.
