TechFest 2026

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a college technology festival created for students who are interested in technology, innovation and new ideas. The event brings students together to learn, participate and showcase their technical skills.

The festival will include coding activities, technology demonstrations, project showcases and interactive sessions. It is a great opportunity for students to learn something new and connect with other technology enthusiasts.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will feature coding challenges, project exhibitions, technology workshops and exciting competitions. Participants can work in teams and present their innovative ideas.

Registration is now open for all interested students. Participants are encouraged to register early and take part in the different activities.

Date and Venue

Date: 15 October 2026
Time: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Venue: GLA University Campus
Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

Speakers

The event will include sessions by technology professionals, faculty members and student innovators. Speakers will discuss current technology trends, career opportunities and practical skills.
