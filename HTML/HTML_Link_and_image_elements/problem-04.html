Jaipur Travel Destination Guide

Jaipur, popularly known as the Pink City, is one of the most beautiful travel destinations in Rajasthan. It is famous for its forts, palaces, culture and delicious food.

1. Attractions

Jaipur has many historical attractions. Some popular places are Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort and City Palace.

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of the most famous landmarks of Jaipur. Its unique windows and pink architecture make it a popular tourist attraction.

Click the Hawa Mahal image to visit its reference page.

Amber Fort

Amber Fort is a historic fort near Jaipur. It is known for its beautiful architecture, large courtyards and royal history.



Learn more about Amber Fort

2. Culture

Jaipur has a rich Rajasthani culture. Visitors can enjoy traditional clothes, handicrafts, folk music and colourful local markets.

3. Food

Jaipur is famous for traditional Rajasthani food. Some dishes worth trying are dal baati churma, gatte ki sabzi, ker sangri and different local sweets.

4. Best Time to Visit

Jaipur is best enjoyed during the cooler months. October to March is the recommended season.

5. Travel Tips

Jaipur can be explored using buses, taxis and auto-rickshaws. Wear comfortable clothes and shoes while visiting tourist places.

Important travel tip: Carry drinking water and keep your important belongings safe while sightseeing.
