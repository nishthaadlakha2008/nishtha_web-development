Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of substances and the changes that happen between them. Some common chemical formulas are:

Water = H2O

Carbon dioxide = CO2

Oxygen = O2

Sulphuric acid = H2SO4

Sodium chloride = NaCl

Atoms and molecules are important topics in chemistry. A chemical formula shows the elements present in a substance.

Physics

Physics deals with matter, energy, motion and forces. The SI unit of area can be written in m2

Speed = Distance / Time

Force = Mass × Acceleration

The value of acceleration due to gravity is about 9.8 m/s2.

Force and motion are important parts of physics. Force can change the motion of an object.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms. Plants, animals and humans are all studied in biology.

Cells are the basic units of living organisms. Every living organism is made up of one or more cells.

Plants use carbon dioxide (CO2) and water (H2O) during photosynthesis. Oxygen (O2) is released during this process.

The human body also needs oxygen for normal breathing and energy production.
