Heavy Rainfall Brings Relief to Greater Noida

Heavy rainfall was reported in Greater Noida on 24 August 2026, giving some relief from the hot weather. Many areas received rainfall during the morning and afternoon hours.

The rain helped reduce the temperature and provided relief to local residents. Several areas received good rainfall during the day. However, some roads also faced waterlogging because of the heavy rain.

People have been advised to travel carefully and avoid roads with heavy waterlogging. Residents were also asked to stay alert during periods of heavy rainfall.

Editor's Note

The rainfall brought relief from the recent heat, but people should still be careful while travelling. Everyone should follow local weather updates for further information.

Students Organise a Cleanliness Drive

A group of students organised a cleanliness drive in their college area. The activity was held on 25 August 2026 to spread awareness about keeping the surroundings clean.

Many students took part in the activity and helped clean the area. The students also collected waste and placed it in proper dustbins. They encouraged other students to keep their surroundings clean.

The activity received a positive response from students and teachers. Such activities can help students understand the importance of cleanliness and teamwork.
